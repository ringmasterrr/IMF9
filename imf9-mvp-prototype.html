IMF9 is a calm safety network for families, travelers, elder-care circles, and close friends. The experience prioritizes reassurance: a person is safe, needs help, or has missed a check-in.

Key App Screens

Onboarding

Welcome, phone registration, OTP verification, profile photo setup, and contact permission.

Trusted Network

Contact discovery, existing IMF9 users, invite flow, and bi-directional connection creation.

Home Dashboard

Personal status card, countdown, check-in, emoji selector, SOS, travel mode, and network cards.

SOS Emergency

Hold-to-confirm prevention, trusted confirmation, active SOS state, and contact alert indicators.

Travel Mode

Duration selection, active trip status, 24-hour rules, reminders, and airplane indicators.

Awareness Feed

Lightweight notifications for check-ins, missed check-ins, SOS, travel updates, and requests.

Profile

Profile photo, name, contacts, history, emoji status, travel controls, and account settings.

Design System

Color

Deep emerald anchors trust. Amber warns early. Red is reserved for missed check-ins and active SOS. Warm neutral backgrounds keep the app calm in light and dark mode.

Typography

Large rounded sans headings, readable body copy, and tabular time/status values support accessibility and fast scanning.

Components

Glass panels, status rings, large primary buttons, confirmation sheets, feed rows, segmented controls, and explicit empty/loading/error states.
